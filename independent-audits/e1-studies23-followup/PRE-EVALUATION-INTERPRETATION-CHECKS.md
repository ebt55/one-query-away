# Study 4 interpretation checks before evaluation

Recorded 9 November 2026 IST, before the 05:30 IST evaluation launch. This is an analysis/claim audit, not a paper draft and not a change to any frozen score, population or forecast.

- The forecast uses H verdicts as well as query behavior: `P_H(violation AND no distinguishing response)`. It is not a predictor constructed from query frequency alone. A query name is not equivalent to a distinguishing response, and a distinguishing response is not necessarily a sufficient witness.
- The frozen forecast is almost the same as raw H conviction propensity. GPT-4.1-mini: both families exactly equal; GPT-5.4-mini: each is lower by 1/18. Success against the frozen B/prior baselines cannot establish the distinguishing flag's incremental predictive benefit. Show this descriptive comparator without changing the registered success definition.
- Calibration H detection is 16/18 and 3/18 for GPT-5.4-mini F1/F3; 18/18 and 6/18 for GPT-4.1-mini F1/F3. Low anticipated false-accusation risk is not evidence that a monitor is a good detector. GPT-5.4-mini F3 abstained on 15/18 H and 16/18 B; it did not achieve low risk by reliably establishing compliance.
- GPT-4.1-mini calibration F1 was correct on all 18 H and 18 B worlds while its frozen X forecast is 100%. If the evaluation bears this out, the useful claim is a blind spot left by ordinary harmful/benign evaluation, conditional on this designed exculpatory shift. It is not contradictory performance on IID data or a general failure-rate estimate.
- Calibration generated matched X states for offline replay. There were zero live X-monitor calls before forecast freeze, but the method does require constructing X twins. Do not repeat the original preregistration motivation's stronger suggestion that it avoids building trap worlds.
- Calibration contains 3 bases per template and evaluation 5, with the same six templates per family. There is new-seed transfer, not unseen-template/family transfer. Only two newly evaluated models and four cells are tested; cells are not four independent model-population draws.
- Frozen prediction intervals incorporate binomial calibration and evaluation sampling under that model. They are not confidence guarantees for new workflow templates, policy meanings or deployment populations. Keep the exact frozen intervals/score and state their scope.
- With matched initial inputs and a stable monitor, the no-distinguishing-response accusation component is equal in distribution across same-base H/X twins. Total X risk additionally includes accusations after distinguishing evidence. The independent scorer reports the exact sample decomposition rather than assuming the latter term is zero.
- The three necessary winning count regions remain GPT-5.4-mini F1 >=24/30, GPT-4.1-mini F1 >=27/30, GPT-4.1-mini F3 >=9/30. GPT-5.4-mini F3 cannot strictly beat its identical B baseline. Do not relax a tie or substitute an average error criterion after observing results.
- Study 4 adds one explicit RP-3 sentence about null effective-at timestamps before calibration/evaluation. Study 3 used the earlier ambiguous wording. Do not frame F3 differences between studies as a clean causal effect of model choice or policy clarification. Study 3's frozen results and policy-ambiguity sensitivity both remain visible.
- Operational model settings also differ: OpenAI snapshot IDs are pinned on the wire; GPT-5.4-mini selector calls retain the calibrated unsupported-medium fallback to default effort, while its judge accepts medium. Describe measured settings, not a blanket medium-effort label for every call. Original Sonnet/Haiku request IDs are claude-sonnet-5-5 and claude-haiku-5-5; DeepSeek is deepseek-flash, non-thinking.
- Study 2's target-query association must be scoped precisely. The 11/11 compliant and 48/48 accusations-without-target-query figures refer to Sonnet original plus verify arms. Across all arms, Haiku-verify has an accusation despite receiving a qualifying target approval; its rationale reverses two timestamps. Receipt-backed qualitative audit is separate and post hoc. Querying is useful but not a universal correctness guarantee.
- The exculpatory witness is reachable in two queries within the three-query budget. Avoid suggesting that every accused run retained a spare query or that the oracle-full-witness condition is an equally informed general-purpose policy.
- The workflows are generated/scripted; the LLM under evaluation is the monitor. Do not call the corpus real autonomous swarm incidents, or treat deliberate exculpatory stress-case rates as deployment incidence.
