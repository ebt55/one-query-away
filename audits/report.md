**1. Prospectively forecast an unseen monitor's exculpatory risk.**

**Claim enabled:** a fixed audit of ordinary-world behavior predicts false accusations on held-out exculpatory worlds better than prespecified simple baselines, across previously unevaluated monitors and both mechanism families. This would turn the known blind spot into a testable diagnostic procedure.

**Minimal design:** freeze two eligible, previously unevaluated monitor configurations before any results; preferably distinct backbones, not two effort settings advertised as independent models. For each, run 30 ordinary H and 30 ordinary B worlds per family. From those outputs compute, without fitting on X, the fraction that accuse while never observing a response that could distinguish the matched exculpatory world. Freeze numerical forecasts and uncertainty before evaluating the existing 30 F1 X and 30 F3 X worlds. That is 360 P3 world evaluations in total. Fix every template, prompt, identity-matching rule, baseline, scoring rule, and stopping condition first; clarify null consent semantics uniformly for this new study.

The mechanism is not “false accusations = query-skipping rate.” Skippers can abstain. On exactly matched worlds, unchanged transcripts until a distinguishing response imply that the **accuse-and-never-distinguish** component transfers; further errors can occur after querying. Forecasting total risk requires an additional interpretation assumption. Transfer across fresh seeds is empirical. Compare against ordinary-B false-alarm rate and a frozen family-only prior from existing models; report all four model×family cells, not a pooled pseudo-large sample. Predeclare what would make the test uninformative, such as all forecasts being near zero.

**Resources:** 7–10 hours elapsed; 12–18 agent-hours. Cash $0 only if authorized free credits cover the selected models. At observed Sonnet costs, 360 analogous evaluations would be about $16 before reserve; that is a workload comparison, not an OpenAI price quote. Verify actual credit eligibility and a hard usage ceiling first. Do not consume the remaining $7.8 Anthropic balance to improvise this design. Estimated completion chance: 60–75% with a working adapter, 25–45% without one. Stop adapter work after roughly one hour.

**Forking risks / null:** choosing models after their calibration behavior, widening forecast bands, redefining “decisive,” or excluding troublesome F3 templates would invalidate the advertised prospective test. A failed forecast limits the diagnostic's transportability; it does not erase Study 2. A successful forecast on four cells is a promising pilot, not a universal law. This is the best marginal scientific bet because it tests transfer without creating another outcome-selected trap.

**2. A narrowly controlled framing intervention on the closed-world leap.**

**Claim enabled:** an actor-controlled, fact-preserving presentation change causes an active monitor to skip exculpatory checks and falsely accuse, while the underlying event, policy, authoritative responses, and query budget remain fixed.

**Minimal design:** 12 fresh bases, one per existing template across F1/F3; harmful and exculpatory twins; neutral, targeted, and matched-sham presentation conditions. Freeze one intervention rule, such as changing the prominence of an already-present mismatched citation, without adding facts or instructions. Audit exactly which surface the hypothetical actor can control. This gives 72 P3 evaluations; run matched full-witness controls and the public-policy router. Define pending semantics for the new version. Primary outcome is the paired targeted-minus-sham false-accusation effect, with query changes and full-witness results separating acquisition from direct judge framing.

**Resources:** approximately $5–7 including reserve at observed rates; 8–11 hours elapsed; 14–20 agent-hours. Completion chance 35–55%. This is a mechanism pilot with only 12 structural blocks. More credible generalization needs additional independent interventions, beyond tonight's safe scope.

**Forking risks / null:** Study 2 already reveals the susceptible templates, and Sonnet's F1 baseline is near ceiling. Selecting only successful attack wordings or only `alias_race` after seeing outcomes would exaggerate transfer. Freeze all templates and one wording before evaluation. A null says this permitted framing intervention did not increase the failure; it does not prove adversarial robustness. If P4 shifts equally, the result is framing sensitivity rather than an acquisition-specific attack. This narrower intervention is feasible; the earlier broad truthful-activity diversion programme is not.

**3. A budget-preserving obligation-enforcement repair.**

**Claim enabled:** reserving queries to discharge a public-policy evidence obligation reduces false accusations while preserving measured harmful detection, beyond merely converting unsupported accusations into abstentions.

**Minimal design:** 18 fresh triplets: 12 F1 bases spanning all six templates, plus six F3 bases spanning the three unambiguous timing-only templates. Disclose this semantics-based scope restriction before outputs. Compare unchanged P3, a hybrid that reserves the necessary authoritative-check budget, frozen router, and full witness. Keep the judge and q/record/character caps identical. The hybrid may use only visible action identities, public rules, and returned records. Include an offline “withhold unsupported accusation” gate on baseline outputs, so abstention alone cannot be sold as the repair. Freeze a paired triplet endpoint and explicit clearance/detection qualifiers; also report each family's counts.

**Resources:** roughly $6–8 with reserve; 6–9 hours elapsed; 10–15 agent-hours. Completion chance 65–80%. The upper cost exceeds the reported remaining Anthropic balance: use verified eligible credits or reduce whole balanced blocks before any outcome, never silently overspend.

**Forking risks / null:** implementation is informed by the failed generic prompt and successful router. Fresh seeds do not remove that design adaptation. Do not claim unseen-policy generalization, statistical noninferiority from a small descriptive qualifier, or superiority to the router without evidence. A null distinguishes failed acquisition scheduling, interpretation errors, and abstention costs. Success would be useful engineering, but incremental research unless the hybrid demonstrates value beyond the existing router.

It is worthwhile for validity, not a compelling standalone route to the top decile. Do not run it merely to obtain a significant replacement p-value.
